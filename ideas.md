# AgroSaarthi — Design Direction

## Three potential approaches

### Theme Name: Field Notes Atelier
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile, editorial farmer companion built from crop-calendar rhythms, paper-like surfaces, and confident agricultural markers. It makes essential information feel calm and close at hand rather than technical or administrative.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Mandi Signal
**Very Brief Intro:** A bold market-facing visual system shaped by price signals, harvest volumes, and expressive data movement. It prioritises momentum and trading confidence while keeping language plain and grounded.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Monsoon Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A cool, rain-led system using deep indigo, rainfall texture, and compact utility panels. It has a dependable, weather-aware personality for a field operating in changing conditions.
**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach: Field Notes Atelier

### Design Movement
**Contemporary agrarian editorialism** — a Framer-inspired, tactile product interface that pairs the practical clarity of a field notebook with the refined pacing of an independent agricultural journal.

### Core Principles
1. **One next step at a time:** A farmer should immediately see the relevant decision and its reason before any complex supporting information.
2. **Trust is visible:** Freshness, uncertainty, source, and connectivity are always shown in plain language, never hidden in a settings screen.
3. **Tactile rather than technical:** Clay, paper, leaf, and field-line motifs replace generic AI imagery and dense dashboard furniture.
4. **Quiet confidence:** Generous breathing room, large labels, high contrast, and compact evidence make the app feel reassuring on a modest mobile device.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a warm, paper-like oat white that feels familiar in bright outdoor light. **Khet Green** anchors decisions and active states; muted marigold marks attention without alarm; rain-blue is reserved for factual weather information. Deep ink text provides legibility against every surface, while earthy terracotta introduces human warmth for crop health and market cues.

### Layout Paradigm
The main desktop experience is an **asymmetric field dashboard**: a persistent, narrow “farm rail” carries identity and navigation; the field journal expands into a fluid content landscape. Cards are intentionally varied in span and hierarchy instead of being a uniform grid. On mobile, the rail becomes a bottom field-path navigation and the hero retains its strong primary action.

### Signature Elements
1. A **contour-line field map** motif that appears as a subtle background pattern and in data cards.
2. **Notched paper cards** with a small agricultural symbol tab at the top edge, used for primary actions and alerts.
3. A vertical **season marker** with date ticks that turns freshness, forecast, and harvest timing into a shared visual language.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like moving through a well-organised field notebook: direct, low-friction, and safely reversible. Primary decisions use large, labelled controls. Status indicators explain themselves in words. Mobile controls have generous targets and all rich transitions are optional for reduced-motion users.

### Animation
Cards make short, 180–240 ms upward fades on entry with 40 ms sequencing. The contour lines drift only at imperceptibly slow opacity shifts and stop entirely under reduced-motion settings. Buttons use a brief 0.97 press scale. Drawers and language menus emerge from their source edge with custom ease-out timing; no looping decorative animation competes with field tasks.

### Typography System
**Fraunces** is the distinctive editorial display face for greetings, key values, and decision headings; it brings agricultural warmth without becoming ornamental. **Manrope** handles all operational text, labels, and data for clarity in English, Hindi, and Marathi. Headlines use confident mixed-case with limited italic emphasis; labels are compact, uppercase, and letter-spaced only when Latin text is used.

### Brand Essence
**AgroSaarthi is the composed, local-language field companion that turns farm signals into the next practical action — online or offline.**

Personality: **grounded, considerate, dependable.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines speak like a knowledgeable farming neighbour: specific, calm, and never overclaiming. CTAs state the actual field action; microcopy exposes data freshness and uncertainty without jargon.

Example lines: “The soil can wait today — check again after Thursday’s rain.”

Example lines: “Your cotton price is moving up. See what this may mean for harvest week.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a bold **sprouting field-line glyph**: three rising contour bands form a leaf and an implied path, suggesting guidance, land, and growth. The wordmark uses a softly inked Fraunces treatment with a custom extended underline that echoes the contour bands. The standalone symbol remains recognisable at app-icon scale.

### Signature Brand Color
**Khet Green — #245D45.** It is the ownable field-green used for direction, confirmations, and the core brand mark.

## Style Decisions

- **Notched paper cards are the default surface for primary decisions and alerts; generic rounded utility panels are intentionally quieter and secondary.**
- **Freshness, forecast, market timing, and harvest windows use one vertical season-marker language wherever timing affects the next action.**
- **Contour-line field texture appears once in every major page section as restrained background grain, a card annotation, or a field-path divider.**
